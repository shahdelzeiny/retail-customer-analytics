# Customer Personality Analysis

A comprehensive multivariate statistical analysis project on customer behavior and marketing performance using the Customer Personality Analysis dataset.

## Project Overview

This project analyzes customer demographics, purchasing behavior, and campaign responses to generate business insights for a retail company.

The analysis combines exploratory data analysis, multivariate statistics, outlier detection, and hypothesis testing to answer key business questions:

- Who are the company's customers?
- Are there abnormal customer profiles affecting the analysis?
- Is the company achieving its business targets?
- Do marketing campaigns significantly impact customer behavior?

---

## Dataset

**Source:** Customer Personality Analysis Dataset (Kaggle)

### Dataset Characteristics
- 2,240 customer records
- Demographic information
- Spending behavior across product categories
- Marketing campaign responses
- Purchase channel information

### Selected Variables
- Age
- Income
- Total Spending
- Web Purchases
- Store Purchases
- Campaign Response

## Key Findings

### Customer Profile
- Higher-income customers tend to spend more.
- Spending behavior varies significantly across customer groups.
- Customer purchasing channels show different engagement patterns.

### Outlier Detection
- Several extreme customer profiles were identified.
- Some outliers may represent data quality issues.
- Others indicate unique customer behavior requiring separate business strategies.

### Campaign Effectiveness
- Customers who accepted marketing campaigns behave significantly differently from those who did not.
- Campaign participation is associated with higher engagement and spending.

---

## Business Recommendations

- Implement customer segmentation strategies.
- Personalize marketing campaigns.
- Monitor and validate extreme customer records.
- Focus on high-value customer groups.
- Optimize campaign targeting based on customer behavior patterns.
